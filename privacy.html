Aroy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Aroy does not collect, track, or share any personal data or usage analytics.

1. Overview

Aroy (“the App”) is an offline educational app for learning Thai dish names, words, and phrases. This policy explains what data the App handles — which is, in short: none. The App is published by MIKO OOO.

  • The App has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The App contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking technologies.
  • The App does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • The App does not share any information with third parties, because it does not collect any.

2. Data stored on your device

Your learning progress, interface language, polite-particle choice, dismissed pronunciation hint, and fact/phrase rotation counters are stored locally on your device only. The App does not transmit this data to us or to anyone else. If you have device backups enabled (for example, iCloud or iTunes backups on iOS), a copy of this local data may be included in that backup; that copy is controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us. Deleting the App removes its local copy; copies in device backups follow your backup provider’s settings and retention rules.

3. Network access

The App works fully offline and makes no network requests to our servers or to any third-party service. The App opens this Privacy Policy page in your browser only when you tap the Privacy Policy button. If you use the Share action, the text you choose to share includes a link back into the App (aroy://) and an App Store or Google Play link; sharing is started by you and handled by the system share sheet. The App itself does not send that content anywhere.

4. Third-party content

The App bundles open-source fonts licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Noto Serif Thai, Onest, Lora, IBM Plex Mono, Orelega One, Caprasimo, and Figtree; the exact set depends on the platform). These fonts are static assets; they do not collect or transmit any data.

5. App Store & Google Play services

The App is free and currently has no in-app purchases. App Store and Google Play downloads, and any diagnostic data Apple or Google collect, are governed by their own privacy policies (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy). The App itself does not receive or process that data.

6. Children

Because the App collects no data from anyone, it also collects no data from children. The App is an educational app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Some cultural content mentions alcoholic drinks; this is educational context, and the App does not promote alcohol.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the “effective date” above will change. Any material change would also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels and the Google Play data safety section before the update ships.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app, email us at aroyappsupport@gmail.com or visit the Support page.

Aroy не собирает, не отслеживает и не передаёт персональные данные или статистику использования.

Aroy («Приложение») — офлайн-приложение для изучения названий тайских блюд, слов и фраз. Эта политика описывает, какие данные обрабатывает Приложение, — коротко: никаких. Приложение публикуется компанией MIKO OOO.

Ваш прогресс обучения, язык интерфейса, выбранная вежливая частица, скрытый совет по произношению и счётчики ротации фактов и фраз хранятся только локально на вашем устройстве. Приложение полностью работает офлайн и не выполняет сетевых запросов ни к нашим серверам, ни к сторонним сервисам. Приложение открывает эту страницу политики в браузере только при нажатии кнопки «Политика конфиденциальности». Поскольку Приложение не собирает данные ни от кого, оно не собирает их и от детей. Приложение носит образовательный характер и не собирает сознательно персональную информацию от детей. 8. Контакты

Die App funktioniert vollständig offline und sendet keine Anfragen an unsere Server oder an Dienste Dritter. Die App öffnet diese Datenschutzseite nur dann im Browser, wenn du auf die Schaltfläche „Datenschutzerklärung“ tippst. Wenn du die Aktion „Teilen“ verwendest, enthält der Text, den du teilst, einen Link zurück in die App (aroy://) und einen Link zum App Store oder zu Google Play; das Teilen startest du, und es wird vom System-Teilen-Menü verarbeitet. Downloads aus dem App Store und von Google Play sowie alle diagnostischen Daten, die Apple oder Google erheben, unterliegen den jeweiligen Datenschutzerklärungen (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy). Die App selbst erhält und verarbeitet diese Daten nicht.

Aroy («la App») es una aplicación educativa sin conexión para aprender los nombres de platos tailandeses, palabras y frases. Esta política explica qué datos trata la App — en pocas palabras: ninguno. La App es publicada por MIKO OOO.

  • La App no tiene cuentas ni inicio de sesión.
  • La App no contiene SDK de analítica, ni publicidad, ni tecnologías de rastreo.
  • La App no solicita acceso a tus contactos, tu ubicación, tus fotos, tu cámara ni tu micrófono.

Al eliminar la App se borra su copia local; las copias de seguridad se rigen por la configuración y la retención de tu proveedor.

La App funciona completamente sin conexión y no realiza peticiones de red a nuestros servidores ni a servicios de terceros.
